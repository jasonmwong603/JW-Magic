FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is the magic family-friendly?

Yes. The comedy is clean, so grandparents and kids can enjoy it right alongside everyone else, and volunteers are always treated with respect.

How much space do you need?

For roving magic, none at all. I come to your guests. For a parlour show, just a spot where everyone can sit or stand and see me, like a living room or a corner of the hall.

When should the magic happen at a wedding?

Cocktail hour is the favourite, since it fills the gap while the couple is away for photos. Between dinner courses works well too. I'll work around your planner or DJ's timeline.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for summer weekends and December. Last-minute requests happen too, so it's always worth asking.

How does payment work?

A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date, and the rest is due on the day after the performance. E-transfer and credit card both work. Full details are in the Terms & Conditions.
