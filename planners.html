Bio & intro.

Short bio

Jason Wong (JW Magic) is an Edmonton magician who picked up his first deck at 15 and has been performing for more than ten years. His clean, comedy-driven card magic, close-up miracles and mentalism have entertained audiences at corporate events, festivals, comedy clubs and private celebrations across Canada.
