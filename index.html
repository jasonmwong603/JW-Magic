Meet your magician

Edmonton's one & only.

I picked up my first deck at 15 and never put it down. Ten-plus years later, I've turned that joy and obsession into a show that I share many across the country.

Card magic, close-up miracles, street magic, gambling demonstrations and mentalism — all delivered with a style that's polished enough for the boardroom and fun enough for the after-party. No top hats. No cringe. Just moments your team will be talking about for years.

  • Clean, fun comedy that works for every audience
  • Fully customizable — logos, products and inside jokes welcome
  • Professional, punctual, and easy to work with from first email to final bow
  • Minimal setup — performs anywhere from boardrooms to ballrooms
