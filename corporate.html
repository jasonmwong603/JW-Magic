FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How long are your sets?

Roving (walk-around) magic is booked in 30-minute or 1-hour increments, so it fits neatly around cocktails, dinner or networking. The stage show is a 60–90 minute set, tailored to your schedule and what you're looking for. Many clients book both.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can — November and December weekends book months ahead. That said, last-minute requests happen all the time, so always ask.

Is the show appropriate for a corporate audience?

Absolutely. The comedy is clean and inclusive, volunteers are always treated with respect, and nobody gets put on the spot in a way they wouldn't enjoy.

What do you need from us on the day?

For roving magic, nothing at all. For stage shows, a clear performance area and a microphone (I can bring my own sound for smaller rooms). I'll send a simple tech sheet after booking.

Can you incorporate our brand or message?

Yes — that's one of my favourite things to do. Logos on cards, product reveals, a prediction that spells out your company tagline. We'll brainstorm on our planning call.

Do you travel outside Edmonton?

Yes. I regularly perform across Alberta and travel anywhere in Canada (and beyond). Travel costs are quoted upfront.

How does payment work?

A deposit secures your date, with the balance due on or before the event. I accept e-transfer, credit card and company cheques, and provide a proper invoice.

Do you perform for private parties too?

Yes! Birthdays, weddings, anniversaries and house parties. Use the same form and just mention it's a private event.
