Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

JW Magic ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through jaywmagic.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a booking request, contact form or mailing-list sign-up, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, company and event details. When you buy merch, payment is processed by our payment provider (e.g. Stripe); we never see or store your full card details.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To plan and deliver booked performances
  • To fulfil and ship merch orders
  • To send mailing-list updates, only if you opted in — unsubscribe anytime

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the business (form delivery, payment processing, shipping), or where required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing jaywmagic@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email jaywmagic@gmail.com.
