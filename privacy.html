Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

JW Magic is a magic and entertainment business operated by Jason Wong in Edmonton, Alberta ("JW Magic", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect through jaywmagic.com and our bookings, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, where it applies, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The short version

  • We only ask for what we need to reply to you, plan your event or ship your order.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We only email you news if you joined the mailing list, and every email lets you unsubscribe.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who is responsible

Jason Wong is responsible for how JW Magic handles personal information. You can reach him at jaywmagic@gmail.com or 780-729-8383.

What we collect, and why

  • Quote, booking and contact emails: your name, email address, any phone number you include, and what you tell us about your event. We use it to reply, prepare a quote and plan the performance.
  • Bookings: if you book, we also keep the event details, invoice and payment records. We need these to deliver the show and keep business and tax records.
  • Mailing list: your email address and the date you signed up. We use it only to send occasional news, show dates and merch updates.
  • Merch orders and lessons: your name, contact details, shipping address and what you ordered, so we can fulfil and ship it. Card payments are handled by our payment provider (Stripe); we never see or store your full card number.
  • Emails and messages you send us: whatever you choose to include.

We don't ask for sensitive information such as government ID, health details or birth dates, and we ask you not to send it.

Consent

By emailing us, booking or ordering, you agree that we can use your information for the purpose it was given. We only add you to the mailing list if you sign up for it yourself; booking or contacting us does not add you. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us, subject to records we must keep by law.

Mailing list and emails

Every newsletter we send says who it is from, how to reach us, and includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribe requests are handled within 10 business days, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Replies about your own enquiry or booking are not marketing and aren't affected by unsubscribing.

Photos and video at events

We often take photos and video at performances for our website and social media, as described in our Terms & Conditions. Guests are told that filming will happen. We never name guests, including children, without permission. If you'd prefer that you, your guests or your event aren't shown, tell us in writing beforehand and we'll respect it. If you or your child appear in something we've posted and you want it taken down, email us and we'll remove it.

Children

This website is meant for adults booking events or buying merch. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Family and birthday shows are booked by a parent or guardian. If you think a child has sent us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Cookies and third-party services

jaywmagic.com does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels, and doesn't set cookies of its own. Because there are no optional trackers to switch on or off, there is no cookie banner. A few outside services load on some pages and may receive your IP address or set their own cookies:

  • Instagram (Meta): the showreel on the home page is an embedded Instagram post. Instagram may set cookies when it loads. See Instagram's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: our fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address. Google states it doesn't set cookies for this. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Formspree: when enabled, mailing list sign-ups are delivered to our inbox through Formspree.
  • Stripe: online card payments take place on Stripe's secure checkout page.
  • Google (Gmail): our email is hosted by Google, so messages you send us are stored there.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site still works without them, although the Instagram showreel may not load.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, a shipping carrier for merch orders, our accountant for tax records, or where the law requires it. These providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it can be subject to the laws of that country.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become bookings: deleted within 2 years.
  • Booking, invoice and order records: kept for 6 years, as Canadian tax law requires.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.

How we protect it

We keep your information in password-protected accounts that only we use. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

See, correct or delete your information

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we've used it;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information, or take you off the mailing list.

Email jaywmagic@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request", or use the contact page. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We'll respond within 30 days, at no cost. If we can't delete something, for example invoices we must keep for tax purposes, we'll tell you what and why.

Concerns or complaints

Please contact us first and we'll do our best to fix it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
